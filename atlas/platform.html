Platform Innovation Centre

Location
Calgary, AB, Canada
Type
Parking / adaptive reuse
Scope
350,000 SF
Practice
5468796 Architecture

The Platform Innovation Centre explores the future of parking structures through a flexible design strategy that anticipates changing transportation needs. Designed as a 510-stall parking garage, the project incorporates a long-term framework for adaptive reuse, allowing the building to transition into office, light industrial, or residential uses over time while enhancing the user experience beyond the conventional parking typology.

My role

  • Supported the design team during Schematic Design and Design Development through research, drawing production, and design iterations.
  • Developed plans, sections, diagrams, and presentation graphics to communicate the project’s adaptive reuse strategy.
  • Assisted in exploring flexible planning concepts that would accommodate multiple future building typologies.
  • Contributed to design studies focused on circulation, user experience, and long-term building adaptability.
  • Collaborated with the project team to prepare presentation materials and design documentation.
