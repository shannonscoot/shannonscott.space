Academic exploration

Social Circus

Location
Bridgeport, Chicago
Type
Performing arts & waterfront
Context
Academic design project

A cross-cultural performing arts center that explores movement as a shared language. Set within a proposal for waterfront revitalization, the project invites people of different ages and backgrounds to learn, train, and perform. Radial portal frames create large open spans around a courtyard; stormwater management, daylight, and acoustics shape the building’s geometry.

My role

  • Concept, program, and architectural design.
  • Structural and environmental systems exploration.
  • Drawings, diagrams, and visualizations.
