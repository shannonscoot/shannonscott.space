Bronzeville Stoop

Location
Bronzeville, Chicago
Type
Housing & community
Context
Academic design project

An ownership-based housing proposal that connects the individual dwelling to the life of the neighborhood. Youth-focused amenities, flexible community spaces, daycare, a grocery store, and locally rooted businesses support families and strengthen community continuity. The proposal brings diverse housing typologies together near the Green Line, honoring Bronzeville’s history of local enterprise and collective investment.

My role

  • Housing and community program development.
  • Site planning, spatial design, and housing typologies.
  • Architectural drawings, visualizations, and presentation.
