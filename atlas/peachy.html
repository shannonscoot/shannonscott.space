Peachy Studios

Location
New York, NY
Type
Aesthetic clinic prototype
Scope
~3,000 SF per location
Practice
Lawrence Group

Peachy Studio is a modern aesthetic clinic that reimagines the Botox experience through hospitality-inspired design, technology, and transparent patient care. Developed as a scalable prototype for multiple New York City locations, each studio integrates clinical functionality with a warm, approachable environment that reflects Peachy’s distinctive brand identity.

My role

  • Led the project from concept through Design Development, collaborating directly with the client to shape the architectural vision and customer experience.
  • Directed client workshops and presentations, developing spatial programming, design concepts, and presentation materials.
  • Designed custom millwork, fixtures, material palettes, and architectural details to establish a cohesive brand identity.
  • Produced Design Development documentation and coordinated with consultants to integrate operational, clinical, and technical requirements.
  • Developed scalable architectural standards and a prototype “kit of parts” to support consistent implementation across future locations.
