Wesbuilt Office

Location
Times Square, New York, NY
Type
Workplace renovation
Scope
~2,000 SF
Practice
718 Design / Freelance

Wesbuilt is a workplace renovation for a Manhattan-based general contractor, designed to support collaboration, flexibility, and day-to-day operations. The tenant improvement reconfigured the office with new reception, meeting, and support spaces, creating a functional environment tailored to the client’s workflow while reflecting their company culture.

My role

  • Co-managed the project from proposal and contract through design, construction, and project closeout, including client relations, invoicing, project administration, and consultant coordination.
  • Developed design concepts, mood boards, material palettes, and finish selections to align with the client’s vision and schedule.
  • Produced Design Development and Construction Documentation while coordinating with millworkers, subcontractors, and vendors.
  • Conducted regular site visits to monitor construction progress, resolve field conditions, and maintain design quality.
  • Collaborated closely with the client to develop a workplace that balanced operational needs, flexibility, and an accelerated project timeline.
