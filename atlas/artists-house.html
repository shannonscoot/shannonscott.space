Artist’s House

Location
Hudson Square, New York, NY
Type
Hospitality / entertainment
Scope
20,000 SF
Practice
Lawrence Group

Artist House is a music incubator and accelerator in downtown Manhattan, bringing together ten state-of-the-art recording studios, artist collaboration spaces, an independent record label, full-service publishing, artist management, and brand partnerships under one roof. The project was designed to foster creativity, collaboration, and high-performance music production within a flexible, hospitality-inspired environment.

My role

  • Collaborated with the Principal to develop the project throughout Schematic Design and Design Development, shaping and refining the design vision.
  • Developed the Schematic Design and Design Development drawing packages, advancing the project from concept through design refinement.
  • Prepared and presented design concepts, drawings, renderings, and visual materials to the Principal and client throughout the design process.
  • Curated interior materials, finishes, and architectural detailing to support the project’s creative vision.
  • Produced plans, elevations, and interior architectural details to communicate design intent and support project coordination.
