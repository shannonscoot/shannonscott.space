Kizik — Short Hills

Location
Short Hills, NJ
Type
Specialty retail
Scope
1,650 SF
Practice
MG2

Located within The Mall at Short Hills, Kizik is a specialty retail store that brings the brand’s innovative hands-free footwear experience into a physical environment. The project translated Kizik’s evolving retail prototype into a cohesive customer experience, integrating branded merchandising, interactive displays, and a flexible store layout that supports product discovery and engagement.

My role

  • Led as Project Architect from Design Development through Construction Administration.
  • Collaborated with the internal design team to refine and implement Kizik’s evolving retail prototype.
  • Produced construction documents for tenant fit-out and landlord approval, incorporating internal QA/QC reviews to improve documentation quality.
  • Conducted site surveys, field verification, and construction site visits to verify existing conditions and support construction.
  • Directed the project permitting process, preparing submissions and coordinating directly with municipal reviewers to secure approvals.
  • Coordinated consultants, contractors, and the landlord to maintain design intent, code compliance, and project schedule.
