Tend is a modern dental concept that reimagines the patient experience through thoughtfully designed clinical and hospitality-inspired environments. Developed as a multi-location prototype across New York and Washington, DC, each practice integrates efficient dental operations with welcoming amenities, creating a consistent brand experience that prioritizes comfort, wellness, and accessibility.

My role

  • Collaborated with the Tend design team and brand consultants to develop architectural standards and a scalable design prototype for multiple East Coast locations.
  • Produced design and construction documentation while coordinating with dental equipment, MEP, and specialty consultants.
  • Developed custom millwork, lighting, and fixture details while value engineering the design to improve constructability, cost efficiency, and consistency across locations.
  • Contributed to prototype refinements and design optimization for the 2024–2025 rollout while maintaining brand standards and operational functionality.
  • Supported site-specific adaptations to meet local code requirements while preserving the overall design intent and customer experience.
