Rundle Housing Development

Location
Calgary, AB, Canada
Type
Affordable housing
Scope
135 residential units
Practice
5468796 Architecture

This affordable housing development provides 135 one-, two-, three-, and four-bedroom homes designed to support individuals and families living on lower incomes. The project emphasizes adaptable living through flexible apartment layouts, allowing residents to customize spaces to accommodate changing household needs over time.

My role

  • Produced interior axonometric drawings in SketchUp to communicate flexible apartment layouts and adaptable living strategies.
  • Developed presentation graphics and Photoshop renderings illustrating multiple occupancy and space-planning scenarios.
  • Created diagrams demonstrating configurable kitchen and bathroom layouts, customizable storage, and multi-functional living spaces.
  • Collaborated with the design team to visually communicate the project’s residential flexibility and user-centered design approach.
  • Prepared graphics and presentation materials supporting the design development process.
