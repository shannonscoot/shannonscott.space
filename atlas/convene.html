Convene — Scholastic

Location
SoHo, New York, NY
Type
Hospitality / events
Scope
32,000 SF
Practice
Design Republic

The second floors of 555 and 557 Broadway were transformed into a unified hospitality destination for Convene within the landmarked Scholastic Building in SoHo. As AOR, the adaptive reuse project translated the design vision into a coordinated, code-compliant meetings and events venue while preserving the building’s historic character. New vertical circulation, a redesigned ground-floor lobby, and enhanced connections between the two buildings created a seamless arrival experience.

My role

  • Collaborated with interior designer throughout Design Development to provide technical guidance, coordinate design intent and facilitate a seamless transition into CDs.
  • Led the production and coordination of Construction Documents for the adaptive reuse of the landmarked building, ensuring compliance with building code, accessibility and permitting requirements.
  • Coordinated multidisciplinary consultants, including structural, MEP, lighting, AV, theatrical, food & beverage.
  • Developed architectural details and resolved existing building conditions to support constructability while maintaining the design intent.
  • Provided Construction Administration through weekly OAC meetings, responding to RFIs, reviewing submittals, resolving field conditions and coordinating with the GC to maintain quality throughout construction.
