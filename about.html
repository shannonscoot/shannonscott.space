Design is a living system that shapes how we move, feel, and connect—from urban grids to digital interfaces to the spaces we inhabit every day. Inspired by the logic of nature, I create work that balances structure with flow, grounding each project in clarity, purpose, and human experience.
At its core, the work is about relationships: between people and place, the built and the natural, and imagination and lived experience. Through this lens, design becomes a form of storytelling—inviting engagement and fostering a sense of belonging.
