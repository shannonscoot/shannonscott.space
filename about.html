Shannon Scott is a designer working across space, image, and form, grounded in a deep connection to nature, ecology, and community. Her work moves between architecture, visual art, and conceptual design, exploring how space shapes human experience.

She approaches each project as a living system—where light, material, movement, and texture are composed to shape how a space is felt as much as how it functions.

At its core, the work is about relationships: between people and place, the built and the natural, and imagination and lived experience. Through this lens, design becomes a form of storytelling—inviting engagement and fostering a sense of belonging.
